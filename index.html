Privacy Policy — Inbox Zero: Survival

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Inbox Zero: Survival ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for your name, email address, or other directly identifying personal information.

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information automatically, including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, network state)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction and app usage data used to measure and serve ads

2. How Information Is Used

Information collected through advertising is used to deliver, personalize (where consented), and measure advertisements, and to detect and prevent fraud and abuse. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising and Consent

The App uses Google AdMob. In regions where required (such as the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland), the App presents a consent prompt via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are shown. You may choose non-personalized ads where that option is offered. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

The App may offer an optional no-ads purchase. When a no-ads entitlement is active, the App suppresses sponsored placements and does not request ads from Google AdMob for that user.

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will address it.

5. Data Retention and Security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Advertising data is retained and secured by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise these rights, adjust the in-app consent prompt or device ad settings, or contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
